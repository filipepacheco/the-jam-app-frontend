## Design Context

### Users

The Jam App serves three groups. Musicians join Performances one-handed from
phones in noisy venues. Hosts make fast, consequential decisions from phones
and desktop consoles. Audiences read the Public Dashboard on shared screens at
a distance. The interface must keep the current Music, Jam state, and next
useful action easy to find.

### Brand Personality

Energetic, social, and precise. The product must feel music-native and
welcoming without becoming playful at the expense of live operational clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the purple/violet brand anchor and semantic status colors with restraint.
Prefer strong hierarchy, deliberate rhythm, and clear type over repeated card
grids. Support both `jam-light` and `jam-dark`. Treat Portuguese as the
reference language and keep English and Spanish robust. Use motion to explain
state changes, with equivalent reduced-motion communication.

### Design Principles

1. Put the current Music and the next useful action first.
2. Adapt density to the context: touch-first for phones, compact for host
   consoles, and large and glanceable for venue displays.
3. Reveal operational complexity progressively and keep live feedback visible.
4. Preserve Jam, Performance, Schedule, Live Queue, Music, and Musician
   behavior while refining presentation.
5. Require deterministic Storybook evidence and human comparison for every new
   or materially changed UI component.
